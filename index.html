Theme 1 · AI across apps
From single features to assistants and automations

Gemini app

Cross-app tasks: Gemini does a multi-step job in the background, like creating a Doc, drafting and sending an email, scheduling a meeting or adding a to-do.Business and Enterprise, Standard and Plus
Skills: instructions you write once and Gemini follows every time. They will replace Gems.
September 2026

Docs

Docs Live: Talk to Gemini and it helps build a structured document, using context from Gmail, Drive, Chat and the web. In the Docs mobile app (Android and iOS).Business and Enterprise, Standard and Plus
October 2026

Workspace Studio

AI agents: Build AI agents that automate work across Workspace, with no code.Business and Enterprise, Education
Webhooks and integrations: connect tools like Salesforce, HubSpot, Jira and Slack (integrations in beta, off by default).Business Starter to Plus; Enterprise Standard and Plus
December 2025 · September 2026
Workspace skills are delayed per Google’s October 13 update. Gems removal is no sooner than March 1, 2027
Theme 2 · Everyday work, faster
Gmail, Slides and Vids

Gmail

AI Inbox: Highlights your to-dos and summarizes priority updates across your email.Business, Enterprise, Frontline Plus
Gmail Live: Ask your inbox a question by voice on mobile and hear the answer, with the source email shown.Business, Enterprise, Frontline Plus, Nonprofits
October 2026

Slides

Deck generation: Describe the presentation and Gemini builds a full, editable deck from your Drive files, styled after a reference deck.Business and Enterprise, Standard and Plus
June 2026

Vids

Document-to-video: A Doc, PDF or Word file becomes a video summary, with an AI-written script, narration and visuals.Business, Enterprise, Education Plus
September 2026

Theme 2 · Everyday work, faster
Sheets, Meet and Chat

Sheets

Sheets canvas: Your data becomes an interactive mini app, like a dashboard or kanban board, built from a plain-language request.Business and Enterprise, Standard and Plus
Formula fixes: One click and Gemini explains and fixes a formula error.Business and Enterprise, Standard and Plus
June · August 2026

Meet

Take notes for me: In person: Gemini captures a meeting in the room and writes a summary, action items and a transcript in a Doc.Business and Enterprise, Standard and Plus; Frontline Plus
Speech translation: English with Spanish, French, German, Portuguese or Italian.Business and Enterprise, Standard and Plus; Frontline Plus
February · August 2026

Chat

Ask Gemini: One box to search your Workspace data, draft messages and catch up on chats. It replaces the Gemini side panel in Chat.Business and Enterprise, Standard and Plus
August 2026
Sheets canvas and Ask Gemini in Chat: English-language accounts only. Speech translation does not include Arabic.
Switching from Microsoft 365
Moving over is easier, and teams keep working

Admin console

OneDrive import: Large-scale file migration from OneDrive, now generally available, at no additional cost.Business, Enterprise Standard and Plus, Education, Frontline, Nonprofit
Import at setup: copy up to 10 Microsoft users and their email, OneDrive files, calendar and contacts when creating the account.All customers, Flexible Plans only
August · September 2026

Calendar

Calendar: Teams, Zoom and Webex links show as a clear “Join video call” button on the event.All Workspace customers
August 2026
OneDrive import has no additional tool cost. Speed depends on the customer’s Microsoft licence tier.
Security and control
Protect the data, and control what the AI can see

Drive

Ransomware detection: Drive spots ransomware and stops syncing, so the damage does not spread.Business Standard and Plus; Enterprise Starter, Standard, Plus; Education Standard and Plus; Frontline Standard and Plus
Bulk file restore: Bring affected files back to their last safe version.All Workspace customers
Data classification: The admin writes plain-language rules and Gemini labels Drive files to match. Open beta.Enterprise Plus; Frontline Plus; AI Pro for Education
March · August 2026

Gmail

End-to-end encryption: Encrypted email that only the sender and the receiver can read, even on another email provider.Enterprise Plus with the Assured Controls add-on
October 2025

Admin console

Business Continuity: Run Workspace in parallel with Microsoft 365, so people keep working during a Microsoft outage. A partner-supported offering, not a new app. Plan and price: confirm with Google.
October 2025
